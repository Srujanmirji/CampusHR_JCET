# Business Rules

## Leave
- Leave balance checked before submission.
- Overlapping requests rejected.
- Approval chain determined by role and leave type.
- Approved leave triggers timetable impact check.

## Substitution
- Candidate must be free during affected slot.
- Candidate must not exceed workload threshold.
- Subject expertise increases recommendation score.
- HOD makes final assignment.

## Attendance
- One attendance record per employee per date.
- Corrections require approval.

## Payroll
- Payroll run is immutable after finalization.
- Corrections require new adjustment entry.
