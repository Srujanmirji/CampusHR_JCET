Faculty recommendations / people: iOS person.2; Android account_circle for an individual and group for a group (group is not needed by current exported screens). Search: magnifyingglass / search. Filters: slider.horizontal.3 / tune. Use only a single family per platform, with an accessible control label; decorative icons are hidden from accessibility.

SF raster exports were generated by export-symbols.swift from this Mac’s system APIs. Use is restricted to Apple platform design review; do not redistribute for Android or non-Apple runtime use. Material font and Inter licensing remain in ../../faculty-screens/assets/.
