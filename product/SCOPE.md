# Scope

## In Scope

- Employee lifecycle
- Department hierarchy
- Attendance
- Leave
- Timetable
- Faculty workload
- Smart substitute recommendation
- Payroll
- Documents
- Notifications
- Reports
- Mobile faculty portal
- Audit logs

## Out of Scope for MVP

- Full student ERP
- Student admissions
- Fee collection
- LMS
- Examination result management
- Hostel management
- Transport GPS
- Parent portal

These may integrate later but should not block HRMS delivery.
